# SUBFLOOR — brand concepts, round 2

Round 1 was rejected and it deserved to be: ten literal diagrams of a line hitting a bar. A price
chart is not an idea. This round has **one idea**, and the logo and the mascot are the same object
seen at two distances.

---

## The idea: the seabed

"Subfloor" already means something before it means anything financial — **the floor beneath the
floor.** The price can sink, and sink, and there is a bottom it cannot pass. That is the sea.

Everything lives at crushing depth, in the dark, under enormous pressure, and is **completely
calm about it**. That is the product: the agent trades however it likes, the pressure comes down,
and the floor does not move. Not because it is armoured — because it *is* the bottom.

This buys three things round 1 had none of:
- **Depth, literally.** Water gives atmosphere, falloff, light from one source. Nothing has to be
  flat.
- **A non-yellow accent that is not borrowed.** Bioluminescence. The only light down there is
  made by the things that live there.
- **A family.** The mascot lives on the floor the logo draws. Same world, same light, same palette.

---

## Palette — no yellow anywhere

- `#05070D` abyssal black — deeper than the UI canvas, this is below it
- `#0B1B2E` → `#132F4C` deep water, for depth falloff
- **`#18C8C8` abyssal cyan — the accent. This replaces brass.** It is the floor, and it is the
  only thing that emits light.
- `#7A5CFF` violet, sparingly, for a second bioluminescent note
- `#E6EDF7` bone white for structure and type
- `#FA2B39` red stays refusal, and appears in *no* brand asset

**Not flat.** Soft volumetric falloff, one light source, gentle grain. Rendered, not diagrammed.
Still must read at 32px — silhouette first, detail second.

## Logos

**L1 — the trench floor**
> Logomark, side view into a deep ocean trench rendered in deep blues `#0B1B2E` to `#132F4C`,
> lit only from below. Two dark canyon walls fall away steeply and meet a perfectly flat seabed
> that glows soft abyssal cyan `#18C8C8` — a horizon line of bioluminescence, the one light in the
> frame. Volumetric, atmospheric depth, fine grain, no hard vector edges. Calm and enormous.
> Square, centred, silhouette legible at 32px. No text.

**L2 — the pressure gauge that will not move**
> Logomark: a heavy deep-sea instrument dial seen face-on, brass-free, machined from dark steel
> with soft specular highlights. The needle is pinned hard at the bottom of its arc against a
> glowing cyan `#18C8C8` stop-pin, unable to travel further. Rendered with real material weight —
> scratches, depth, one light source from upper left. Deep blue-black background `#05070D`.
> Square, centred, works at 32px.

**L3 — the anglerfish lure as a mark**
> Logomark: an extreme close-up of a single bioluminescent lure — a glowing cyan `#18C8C8` orb on
> a curved dark filament, emerging from black water `#05070D`. The orb casts real light into the
> surrounding water, falling off into darkness. Nothing else is visible. Mysterious, minimal,
> volumetric. The whole mark is one point of light in the dark. Square, 32px silhouette.

**L4 — the sounding line**
> Logomark: a plumb line dropping through dark water, the weight coming to rest on a flat seabed
> that glows faint cyan `#18C8C8` where it touches. The rope's slack curls slightly — it has
> reached bottom and stopped paying out. Deep water gradient `#0B1B2E` to `#05070D`, soft
> godrays from far above, fine grain. Quiet, nautical, precise. Square, centred.

**L5 — the monogram, submerged**
> Logomark: the letter **S** carved from dark stone, sitting on a seabed, seen three-quarter, half
> buried in sediment. Bioluminescent cyan `#18C8C8` algae traces the lower edge where the stone
> meets the floor, glowing softly into the water. Ancient, load-bearing, unbothered. Volumetric
> lighting, real material, deep blue-black water. Square, centred, silhouette legible at 32px.

## Mascots

Same world, same light, same palette. **Deadpan, never cute.** It is not guarding the floor; it
*is* at the floor, and nothing that happens above concerns it.

**M1 — the anglerfish**
> Mascot: a deep-sea anglerfish, front-three-quarter, resting on the seabed in near-black water
> `#05070D`. Its lure glows abyssal cyan `#18C8C8` and is the only light source, illuminating its
> face from above and falling off into dark. Expression flat, patient, faintly bored. Stylised and
> characterful, not scientific illustration; volumetric, soft grain, real depth. Square.

**M2 — the abyssal isopod**
> Mascot: a giant deep-sea isopod, armoured segmented plates rendered with real material depth,
> sitting squarely on a glowing cyan `#18C8C8` seabed line. Low, wide, immovable, deadpan. Lit
> from below by the floor itself so the plate edges catch the light. Deep blue-black water,
> volumetric falloff, fine grain. Characterful but never cute. Square.

**M3 — the sea spider surveyor**
> Mascot: a long-legged deep-sea sea spider standing over a flat glowing cyan `#18C8C8` seabed,
> legs braced wide, one limb extended as if measuring the floor's level. Delicate but entirely
> steady. Dark translucent body catching the floor's light. Volumetric, atmospheric, deep blues.
> Square, silhouette readable small.

**M4 — the tripod fish**
> Mascot: a tripod fish standing on three long stiff fins on the seabed, elevated slightly above
> it, facing the current. Body dark and slender, fins catching cyan `#18C8C8` light from the
> glowing floor beneath. It has stood exactly here for a very long time. Calm, absurd, dignified.
> Deep water, volumetric light, soft grain. Square.

**M5 — the diver at the bottom**
> Mascot: a stocky vintage brass-free deep-sea diver in a dark steel helmet, standing flat-footed
> on a glowing cyan `#18C8C8` seabed, arms relaxed at sides. The helmet's small round port glows
> faintly cyan from within. Enormous pressure, complete composure. Rendered with material weight
> and volumetric water light, deep blue-black. Square, silhouette legible at 32px.
