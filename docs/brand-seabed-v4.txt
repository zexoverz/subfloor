TEN SEPARATE ASSETS ON A FULLY TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND (PNG with alpha).
Row 1 = five icons L1..L5.  Row 2 = five mascots M1..M5.

BACKGROUND — the most important instruction:
Transparent. Alpha channel. NO background at all. NO scenery, NO water, NO ocean,
NO seabed, NO ground, NO floor, NO rocks, NO godrays, NO light shafts, NO bubbles,
NO fog, NO vignette, NO frame, NO panel, NO card behind the subject.
Each subject is cut out and isolated, floating alone on nothing.

STYLE:
Clean cartoon sticker illustration. Cel shading only — two or three flat tones per
colour with hard-edged shadow shapes, plus one small highlight. Bold even outline
around every subject. Simple chunky geometry, no fine detail, no texture, no grain,
no photorealism, no rendered 3D, no cinematic lighting, no depth of field.
Looks like a modern app mascot sticker pack, not a movie frame.

COLOUR — no yellow, gold, amber or brass anywhere:
navy #132F4C and #0B1B2E for bodies, bright cyan #18C8C8 for the single glowing
element on each subject, bone white #E6EDF7 for eyes and highlights.
The glow is a simple flat cyan shape, not a rendered light source.

L1..L5 — ICONS. One bold simple shape each, wide margins, readable at 32x32 px:
L1 a thick rounded bar with a flat cyan glow strip on top, one simple droplet on it
L2 a round gauge, needle pointing straight down onto a cyan stop at the bottom
L3 a cyan orb on a short curved stalk
L4 a plumb-bob teardrop on a short line, tip touching a cyan bar
L5 a chunky rounded letter S, lower half cyan

M1..M5 — MASCOTS. Full body, front or three-quarter, standing pose, no ground beneath
them. Big simple eyes, friendly and deadpan, one consistent character family:
M1 chubby anglerfish, big cyan lure bulb, tiny fins, goofy underbite
