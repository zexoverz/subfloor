SUBFLOOR — deep-sea parallax hero. Eight assets, generated one at a time.

READ FIRST — these apply to every single asset:

PALETTE (sampled from our logo and mascot, do not substitute):
  #041224 deepest navy   #061c34 dark navy   #113860 mid navy
  #18406e lighter navy   #0ee6ea cyan (the only light)   #e9f1fb bone white
  NO yellow, NO gold, NO amber, NO orange, NO purple, NO red anywhere.

STYLE: clean cel-shaded cartoon illustration matching a cute mascot sticker set.
  Flat tones with hard-edged shading, bold simple shapes, no photorealism, no
  texture, no grain, no film look, no lens flare, no depth-of-field blur.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent PNG with alpha. No background fill, no frame, no
  border, no vignette, no watermark, no text, no labels.

DEPTH RULE — the single most important instruction, and it differs per layer:
  Layers meant to sit FAR AWAY must be LOW CONTRAST and close in value to the
  water: pale, washed, almost silhouettes. Layers meant to sit NEAR must be DARK
  and high contrast with crisp edges. This value difference is what creates the
  depth; without it the parallax reads as flat cut-outs sliding around.

---

ASSET 1 — light shafts (farthest, moves least)
Wide panoramic image 2400x900, transparent PNG. Soft godrays angling down from
upper left to lower right, rendered as straight-edged translucent cyan #0ee6ea
wedges at very low opacity, like flat cel-shaded light beams. Six to nine beams
of varying width, widely spaced, fading out completely before the bottom edge.
Extremely subtle and pale. No glow bloom, no rays crossing, nothing else in frame.

ASSET 2 — marine snow, SEAMLESSLY TILEABLE
Square 1200x1200, transparent PNG, and it MUST tile seamlessly — the left edge
continues into the right edge and the top into the bottom, with no seam and no
element clipped at any border. Scattered tiny specks and short soft flecks of
pale bone white #e9f1fb and cyan #0ee6ea at low opacity, evenly distributed with
irregular natural spacing, like drifting particles suspended in water. Simple flat
dots and dashes, no sparkle shapes, no stars, no bokeh circles.

ASSET 3 — far trench walls (background silhouette)
Wide panoramic 2400x1000, transparent PNG. The silhouettes of two distant rocky
canyon walls, one entering from the left edge and one from the right, leaving a
wide empty gap of transparency between them. Flat single-tone shapes in pale mid
navy #113860 at reduced opacity — nearly a flat silhouette, almost no internal
detail, only two or three simple value steps. Soft rounded rock forms, cartoon
geology, not realistic stone. Bottom of the image is empty transparency.

ASSET 4 — mid-ground rock outcrops
Wide panoramic 2400x800, transparent PNG. A row of chunky rounded boulders and
low rock shelves along the bottom third, in dark navy #061c34 with simple cel
shading in #113860 on their upper surfaces. Thin cyan #0ee6ea bioluminescent
moss traces the top edge of a few rocks only, not all of them. Irregular heights,
generous gaps between groups, transparent above. Cartoon shapes, bold and simple.

ASSET 5 — the seabed floor, SEAMLESSLY TILEABLE HORIZONTALLY
Wide strip 2400x400, transparent PNG. The left and right edges MUST match exactly
so the strip can repeat horizontally without a visible seam. A gently undulating
sandy seabed occupying the lower half, in deepest navy #041224, with the very top
edge of the sand lit by a continuous soft cyan #0ee6ea bioluminescent glow line.
Sparse tufts of simple cyan-tipped seagrass. Everything above the sand is
transparent. Flat cel shading, no texture detail.

ASSET 6 — foreground kelp, LEFT edge
Portrait 900x1400, transparent PNG. A cluster of tall cartoon kelp fronds rising
from the bottom left corner, leaning right, drawn as near-black navy #041224
silhouettes with a thin cyan #0ee6ea rim on the left side of each frond only.
High contrast, crisp bold edges, very dark — this sits closest to the viewer.
Right two-thirds of the canvas fully transparent. Simple ribbon shapes, no detail.

ASSET 7 — foreground kelp, RIGHT edge
Portrait 900x1400, transparent PNG. Mirror companion to asset 6: a cluster of
tall cartoon kelp fronds rising from the bottom right corner, leaning left, in
near-black navy #041224 silhouette with a thin cyan #0ee6ea rim on the right side
of each frond only. Same weight and darkness as its pair. Left two-thirds fully
transparent.

ASSET 8 — drifting bubbles, SEAMLESSLY TILEABLE
Square 1000x1000, transparent PNG, tiling seamlessly on all four edges with
nothing clipped at the borders. Scattered simple cartoon bubbles of assorted
small sizes: thin cyan #0ee6ea outlined circles with one small bone white #e9f1fb
highlight dot each, hollow centres, low opacity. Irregular spacing, no clusters,
no rainbow sheen, no gradients.
